Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

What this website does

ATS Tracker Pro analyzes resume text and, when provided, a job description. The website is designed to process resume content in your browser.

Resume and job-description data

Resume files and pasted text are processed locally by the browser for the analysis and export features. Do not upload information you are not authorized to use. The optional server-side tailoring endpoint, if enabled by the site operator, is described in the site configuration and should be reviewed before use.

Storage

The application may use browser storage to preserve an in-progress session on your device. You can clear this data through your browser settings.

Third-party services

The website may load document-processing libraries required for PDF and DOCX extraction. No third-party analytics or advertising claim is made by this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the website operator through the published contact method for the connected custom domain.
