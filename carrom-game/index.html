How to Play Carrom Pool

Objective: Pocket all of your assigned color discs (White or Black) plus the Red Queen before your opponent does!

Controls:

  • Position Striker: Drag the bottom slider to place your striker along the baseline.
  • Aim & Power: Click/touch and drag backwards from the striker to set aiming direction and shot power.
  • Shoot: Release your mouse or finger to release the striker!

Rules & Scoring:

  • White Disc: 10 Points
  • Black Disc: 5 Points
  • Red Queen: 30 Points (Must pocket your own disc to cover the Queen or score extra)
  • Foul (Striker Pocketed): Lose a turn and suffer a penalty point deduction (-5 pts).
